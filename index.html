FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Quizify?
Quizify is an AI-powered study platform that helps students review by generating quizzes and flashcards from uploaded PDF modules.
How do I start studying?
Simply upload a PDF module, wait for the AI to process it, then start answering quizzes or reviewing flashcards.
What file format can I upload?
Quizify currently supports PDF files containing your study modules or lecture materials.
How long does it take to generate quizzes?
Generation usually takes only a few moments, depending on the size and complexity of your uploaded PDF.
Can I generate flashcards too?
Yes. Quizify automatically creates flashcards from your uploaded module to help you memorize important concepts.
Does Quizify save my progress?
Yes. Your completed quizzes, study streak, and learning activity are automatically saved to your account.
How are my dashboard statistics calculated?
Your dashboard reflects your actual study activity, including completed quizzes, flashcards reviewed, scores, and study streak. Quizify does not generate or estimate results.
Can I retake a quiz?
Yes. You can retake quizzes anytime to improve your understanding and track your progress.
What happens if I miss a day?
Missing a day without completing at least one quiz will reset your study streak to zero.
Can I upload the same PDF again?
Yes. You may upload the same module again if you want to regenerate study materials or review the content.
What if my PDF cannot be processed?
Ensure that your PDF is readable, contains selectable text, and is not password-protected. Scanned images may not generate accurate study materials.
Can I access my uploaded modules later?
Yes. Your uploaded modules remain available in your account, allowing you to review them again whenever needed.
Is there a limit to the number of PDFs I can upload?
The upload limit depends on the system's current storage policy. If a limit is reached, you may need to remove older modules before uploading new ones.
Can I use Quizify on my phone?
Yes. Quizify is designed to work on desktops, tablets, and most modern mobile browsers.
Is my uploaded data secure?
Yes. Your uploaded modules and study records are stored securely and are only accessible through your account.
